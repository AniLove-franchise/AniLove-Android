Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026 · applies to AniLove for Android, version 1.3 and later

AniLove is an Android client for AniList. It is built and published by one independent developer. There is no AniLove server. No account, database, analytics service or crash reporter is operated for this app, and none of your data is sent to the developer.

The short version. Your AniList account is handled by AniList. Your pictures are handled by your own Google Drive, encrypted before upload. Everything else lives on your phone. The developer never receives, sees or stores any of it.

1. What is stored on your device

No contacts, calendars, SMS, call logs, location, camera, microphone, photos library or device identifiers are read. Pictures are picked through Android's own photo picker, which hands the app only the single image you select.

2. What goes to AniList

Signing in uses AniList's OAuth page. The app never sees your AniList password. Your token is then used to read and write your lists, activity, notifications and messages — which means AniList receives those requests along with your IP address. AniList stores your messages and profile according to AniList's own terms and privacy policy. Deleting AniLove does not delete your AniList account or messages; you can revoke the app at anilist.co/settings/apps.

3. What goes to Google (only if you connect Drive)

Sending a picture is optional. If you connect Google Drive, AniLove requests one permission: access to files the app itself creates (drive.file). It cannot list, read or modify anything else in your Drive.

  • Your Google email address is read once so the app can show which account is connected.
  • The picture is encrypted on the phone before it is uploaded, and the encrypted file is placed in your Drive.
  • The file is marked "anyone with the link can view" so the person you are messaging can download it without a Google account. Because it is encrypted, a link by itself reveals nothing readable.
  • Google never receives the decryption key. AniList does — see section 5.

You can disconnect Drive in the app at any time, delete the files in your Drive like any other file, or revoke access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Google's handling of the file is covered by Google's privacy policy.

5. Encryption, in plain language

What it protects: Google, link scanners, and anyone who finds the link. They only ever get unreadable bytes.

What it does not protect: AniList and the person you are talking to. The key travels inside the chat message, and AniList stores that message. This is encryption for storage and transport, not end-to-end encryption against the chat service.

Pictures are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a fresh random key per file. The key is placed in the part of the URL after #, which browsers and servers never send anywhere. Before upload, photos are re-encoded, which also removes EXIF metadata such as camera model and GPS coordinates. Animated GIFs are sent as they are.

6. What the developer never receives

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reports, advertising IDs or usage statistics.
  • No AniList token, Google token, message, picture or file list.
  • No account of any kind is created for AniLove.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with advertisers — there is nothing to share.

7. Security measures

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS; cleartext HTTP is disabled at the Android platform level.
  • Android cloud backup is disabled for the app, so tokens are not copied off the device by backup.
  • Tokens are kept in app-private storage, separated per profile, and never written to logs.
  • The app's WebView cannot read device files, and page scripts cannot reach the native bridge from any origin other than the app itself.
  • Every release is signed with the same certificate; Android refuses to install an update signed by anyone else.

No system is perfect. If you find a security problem, please report it privately through the repository's issue tracker.

8. Your controls

  • Delete messages in the app — they are removed on AniList for both sides.
  • Delete pictures in Google Drive, or disconnect Drive in the app.
  • Revoke AniList access at anilist.co/settings/apps.
  • Revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Remove everything local by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

9. Children

AniLove is not directed at children under 13 and requires an AniList account, which itself has a minimum age. The app does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone — there is no server to collect it on.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what leaves your device, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. The app never adds a new third-party service silently.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue in the AniLove repository.

AniLove is an unofficial client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by AniList, Google, Tenor or MyAnimeList.
